## Brand Guidelines

### Colors

**Primary Palette:**
- Blue: `#2563eb` (from-blue-600)
- Purple: `#9333ea` (to-purple-600)
- Indigo: `#4f46e5` (indigo-700)
- Yellow Accent: `#fbbf24` (yellow-400)

**Gradients:**
- Main Gradient: `from-blue-600 via-purple-600 to-indigo-700`
- Text Gradient: `from-blue-600 to-purple-600`

### Logo

The Trainer One logo consists of:
1. A gradient-filled rounded square with a **Brain** icon
2. A small yellow **Zap** icon in the bottom-right corner (representing speed/power)

### Typography

- **Display Font**: System default with font-bold
- **Tagline**: "Professional AI Model Fine-tuning Platform"
- **Alternate**: "Professional-grade LLM fine-tuning made simple"

### Voice & Tone

- **Professional** yet **Accessible**
- **Technical** but **User-friendly**
- Emphasize **Speed**, **Efficiency**, and **Quality**

### Key Messages

1. "Make professional-grade LLM fine-tuning accessible and simple"
2. "Built on Unsloth's optimized training pipeline"
3. "Train AI models like a pro"
4. "From data to deployment, simplified"
